LingXi -- AI Spirit Pet x Eastern Fortune Telling

All things possess a spirit. LingXi merges millennia of Eastern metaphysical wisdom with cutting-edge AI to create your very own Spirit Pet companion. Enter your birth date and time, and the app matches you with one of 24 unique Spirit Pets based on the Chinese solar terms. Your Spirit Pet accompanies you every day -- reading fortunes, guiding directions, analyzing your face -- serving as your personal mystical life advisor.

--- 24 Solar Term Spirit Pets ---

Based on your birthday, the system assigns one of 24 Spirit Pets: the Spring Deer of Lichun, the Summer Lion of Xiazhi, the Autumn Tiger of Liqiu, the Winter Dragon of Dongzhi... Each pet carries a unique Five Elements attribute and personality. Through daily interaction, your pet levels up and undergoes three evolution stages at Lv.10, Lv.20, and Lv.30, unlocking new forms and abilities.

--- Five Fortune-Telling Systems ---

LingXi integrates five Eastern and Western divination systems for comprehensive life guidance:

- BaZi (Eight Characters): Four Pillars of Destiny analysis based on your birth year, month, day, and hour
- Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology): Twelve-palace destiny chart covering career, love, and wealth
- Qi Men Dun Jia (Mystical Door Escape): Real-time Eight Gates directional guidance for daily auspicious positioning
- Western Astrology: Sun sign analysis cross-referenced with Five Elements for a dual East-West perspective
- Sixty Jiazi Oracle Lots: Shake your phone to draw oracle lots, cast moon blocks for confirmation, and let your Spirit Pet interpret the result
